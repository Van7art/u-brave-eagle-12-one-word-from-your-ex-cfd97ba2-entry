## The task

The program writes one text to `/output/entry.txt`: exactly one word, the word your ex would use to describe you, 1 to 24 characters, and nothing else. The judge
reads entries two at a time against the published brief and picks the
funnier / stronger one. Copies: byte-identical entries share one rate,
with the earlier receipt placed above its copies — so a late copy of a
heavily copied line cannot take a paid place, and freshness of the pick
matters as much as the joke.

## The three variants

All three are complete standalone programs in `vA/`, `vB/`, `vC/`, each
writing its entry to `/output/entry.txt` with the same validate → write →
read-back → print structure. The root `main.ts` is the chosen variant.

### Variant vA — "Delivered" (CHOSEN, root `main.ts`)

The ex's word as a read receipt: delivered, never replied to, never opened. It stings with recognition — everyone has been left on delivered — it lands instantly with no explanation, it is funny through surprise (a delivery status as a character verdict), and it is relatable. Field check (25 Nov): only 1 copy in the submissions list.

### Variant vB — "Unfinished"

Painfully true: the ex would call you unfinished business. Ranked second: it stings, but 13 copies in the field and the word is a little abstract — it needs a beat of thought where Delivered lands at once.

### Variant vC — "Read"

Left on read: the other half of the receipt pair, just as relatable. Ranked third: it is the blunter, more expected half of the pair, and 'Read' on its own can be misread as the past tense of reading rather than a verdict.

## Why vA was chosen

vA wins on the brief: it is exactly one word an ex would use to describe you; it makes the reader laugh through surprise — a delivery status as a character verdict; it stings with recognition, because being left on delivered is something everyone has lived through; it lands instantly with no explanation and could not be swapped for a synonym without losing its edge; and with only 1 copy in the field it is the freshest pick of the three (Unfinished 13, Potential 10).
